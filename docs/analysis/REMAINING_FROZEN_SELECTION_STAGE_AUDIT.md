## 3. numu: source and model
(`FROZEN_NUMU_BDT_SOURCE_AUDIT.md`.) XGBoost-converted TMVA BDT (`xgboost2TMVA`, `Grad`), **300 trees, depth 5, 8,403 leaves**, all 73 inputs used, sha256 `5886affb...` identical to the MicroBooNE reference bundle, score `tanh(sum)` with margin threshold 0.4236. The 73 inputs: shower quality (br1/br3/lem) 29, vertex/gap geometry 11, calorimetry 10, numuCC-tagger flag-3 inputs 8 (`numu_cc_3_*`), track/hadron activity 7, multiplicity 6 and **two upstream BDT outputs** (`numu_1_score`, `numu_score`). Defaults: the `numu_cc_*` family is at its struct default for 69.8 % of the events (flag 3: 98.6 %), the track/hadron family for 81.0 %, the shower-quality family 14.7 %; the upstream scores are never at default.

Stage survival **62.3 %** (5,007 / 8,038; 68 % interval 61.7-62.8 %): truth 0p **62.1 %**, truth 1p **63.0 %**, truth >= 2p **59.4 %**; Delta0 61.4 %, Delta+ 63.5 %. By reconstruction state: **clean signal-photon shower 76.4 %** vs **non-clean photon object 33.6 %** (fragmented 36.0 %, typed non-electron 21.9 %, track 28.0 %, row missing 48.2 %); **vertex anchored 78.0 % vs not anchored 56.1 %**; **true reco proton present 75.5 % vs absent/other 59.1 %** (anchored = |V_reco - V_true| <= 5 cm). ## 5. numu: reconstruction-state dependence and the main drivers
* **Attribution (exact interventional TreeSHAP of the margin of 3,031 rejected against 5,007 accepted signal candidates; 600 / 300 sampled; additivity error 1.6e-14; margin gap -2.84):** by family: **shower quality (br/lem) 44.4 % of the |phi| mass, mean contribution -1.05**; **the upstream `numu_score` + `numu_1_score` 21.5 %, mean -1.30** (`numu_score` alone: -1.29, the single largest variable); vertex/gap geometry 10.1 % (-0.25); multiplicity 8.1 %; numuCC-tagger inputs 6.6 %; calorimetry 6.2 %; track/hadron 3.1 %. Top variables: `numu_score`, `shw_sp_lem_e_charge`, `br1_2_n_shower_segs`, `br3_1_n_shower_segments`, `br1_1_n_segs`. Truth 0p and truth 1p rejected events have the same attribution pattern (-1.17 / -1.44 upstream, -1.10 / -1.01 shower quality). (`numu_attribution_*.csv`, `numu_attribution.png`.)
* **What the rejected objects look like** (pass vs fail, median, `input_comparison_top8_per_pair.csv`): `numu_score` 0.12 (rejected) vs -0.81 (accepted); **number of shower segments 5 vs 14, total shower length 21 vs 56 cm, `lem_e_charge` 82 vs 226**: the BDT rejects **compact, few-segment shower objects** (muon-like in its training) and keeps extended, many-segment showers. The same variables separate clean from non-clean photon objects even more (total shower length 17 vs 59 cm, SMD 1.3).
* **Hadronic activity:** survival is **45.5 % when the candidate contains a muon/pion-typed row** and **83.3 % when it does not** (clean photons only: 62.2 % vs 85.7 %); by number of track rows 0: 70.9 %, 1: 67.4 %, 2: 45.0 %, 3-4: 34.6 %, 5+: 36.5 % (`numu_survival_by_driver.csv`).
* **Not an energy effect:** survival is flat in the true photon energy: 100-200 MeV 60.5 %, 200-300 62.4 %, 300-400 64.6 %, 400-600 62.4 %, 600+ 56.7 %; flat in the true photon beam angle (59-64 %); defaulted inputs (0-20 / 20-30 defaulted of 73) 64.8 % vs 59.3 %. For photons above 400 MeV the failure fraction (599 of 1,556) is 38.5 % against 37.7 % for the whole population.
* **Topology (Task 18):** the raw truth-1p minus truth-0p survival difference is +0.9 pp; adjusting for the reconstruction state it becomes -2.3 pp (+ photon cleanliness), -7.4 pp (+ vertex anchoring), **-10.2 pp (+ true reco proton present)**, -10.1 pp with energy and direction: **the apparent equality of 0p and 1p is a cancellation between a reconstruction-state advantage of truth-1p (more anchored vertices, more true protons, which favour survival) and an intrinsic disadvantage of -10 pp** (a proton track brings muon/pion-like objects); truth >= 2p minus 0p: -2.7 -> -12.7 pp; Delta+ minus Delta0: +2.1 -> -3.7 pp (`topology_decomposition.csv`; observed-association bookkeeping, not causal).

## 7. ncpi0: signal rejection (`ncpi0_score_distributions.png`, `ncpi0_attribution.png`, `ncpi0_em_topology_classes.png`)
Stage survival **47.2 %** (1,313 / 2,781): truth 0p 49.6 %, truth 1p 47.1 %, truth >= 2p 36.7 %; Delta0 44.8 %, Delta+ 49.6 %. **Attribution** (1,465 rejected vs 1,313 accepted, 600 / 300; margin gap -1.15; additivity 4.9e-15): **pi0 topology 46.5 % of the |phi| mass (mean -0.46; `kine_pio_angle`, `kine_pio_energy_2`, `kine_pio_mass`, `shw_sp_pio_*`)**, upstream sub-scores 24.1 % (-0.26; `stw_3_score`, ...), multiplicity 12.8 % (-0.16), shower quality 9.3 %, `E_indirect_max_energy` 7.3 % (-0.15).
**What does it reject?** The sample has exactly **one true photon above 25 MeV in every event** (`n_gamma25 = 1`), so **no rejected event is a true pi0 pair above 25 MeV**; every rejection is a single-photon event for which the reconstruction forms a shower pair (see the Phase-14 correction below the table: only the tagger-side pair is pi0-mass-like). By EM topology class of the controlling candidate (exclusive hierarchy; `ncpi0_em_topology_classes.csv`):

| class | events | rejected | survival | share of the rejected |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| kinematics-tree pi0-pair candidate only (`kine_pio_flag != 0`, no tagger-side pair) | 1,743 | 1,137 | 34.8 % | **77.5 %** |
| reco pi0 pair, mass within 60 MeV of 135 | 267 | 243 | **9.0 %** | 16.6 % |
| single energetic shower, no pi0-pair flag | 669 | 38 | **94.3 %** | 2.6 % |
| signal photon split over several shower rows | 87 | 41 | 52.9 % | 2.8 % |
| other energetic shower in the candidate | 11 | 6 | 45.5 % | 0.4 % |
| reco pi0 pair, other mass | 4 | 3 | 25.0 % | 0.2 % |

So the `ncpi0` stage rejects signal whose photon the pattern recognition **pairs with a second object** into a pi0-like candidate (a fragment of the same photon, a hadronic shower or an unrelated shower): a **tagger artifact on a genuine single photon**, not a pi0. Signal with no pair flag survives at 94 %.

> **Correction (Phase 14, `NCPI0_PAIR_MEMBER_TRUTH_AUDIT.md`; the numbers above are unchanged).** The sentence above is too strong. Measured event by event: (i) the *kinematics-tree* pair (`kine_pio_*`, 77.5 % of the rejected) is the **largest-`E1 + E2` shower pair with no mass requirement**, not a pi0 candidate: **74.5 %** of the kine-only pairs have a stored mass < 100 MeV (median 47.7 MeV) and only 6.9 % lie in 100-170 MeV; (ii) only the *tagger-side* pair (`shw_sp_pio_*`, 246 rejected events = 16.8 %) is a mass-window pair, and it always contains the selected shower; (iii) of the 1,468 rejected events **94.2 %** have a pair flag and **88.0 %** a pair that contains the signal photon (dominant object or a piece of it), 5.8 % have no pair flag; (iv) the second member is, for clean photons rejected with a kine pair, **other signal-interaction EM (32 %, neutron-capture-like low-energy photons), a piece of the same photon (30 %), a signal proton/hadron (20 %)**, cosmic 7.5 %; for the tagger pair **a piece of the same photon (43 %) and a signal proton/hadron (36 %)**; unrelated neutrino interactions are minor. "Pairs with a second object into a pi0-like candidate" is therefore true of the tagger-side pair, and for the kine pair only in the sense that a second detached shower row exists. Event-level fragmentation (Phase 9) explains 8.6 % of the rejections (126 of 1,468).


## 8. ncpi0: reconstruction-state dependence
Survival: **clean signal-photon shower 50.1 % vs non-clean 14.7 %** (fragmented 16.0 %, typed non-electron 6.9 %, track 25.0 %); **anchored 51.2 % vs not anchored 43.0 %**; true reco proton present 52.2 % vs absent 44.3 %; proton origin: Delta-decay protons 52.7 %, FSI-created 35.3 % (truth-1p events); candidate multiplicity: only 2 events with two reaching candidates. Even a **clean** photon is rejected in half of the cases. The availability of the sub-BDT scores depends on the vertex state: **for anchored events 79 % of `lol_1_score`, 79 % of `sig_2_score` and 81 % of `br3_6_score` sit at their scorer defaults, against 17-37 % for not-anchored events**, and the non-default values are lower (median `lol_1_score` 0.56 vs 0.85): which sub-taggers fire is a reconstruction-state effect. Topology after adjustment for the reconstruction state: truth 1p minus 0p -2.5 pp raw -> **-13.8 pp** (+ true reco proton), truth >= 2p -13.0 -> -10.6 pp (the last two adjustments are singular for 2 events), Delta+ minus Delta0 +4.9 -> +2.9 pp.

## 9. nue stage
(`FROZEN_NUE_BDT_SOURCE_AUDIT.md`.) 300 trees, depth 5, 56 `shw_sp_*` inputs, no upstream score. The cut `> -1.0` is **vacuous by construction** (a `tanh` of a finite margin lies in (-1, 1); lowest score -0.999998): 1,313 enter, 1,313 survive. ## 10. n_20br1: source semantics
(`N20BR1_SEMANTICS.md`.) Counts the electron-typed showers of the **main vertex's shower set** with `kine_best > 20 MeV` and `badreco1` (no long muon-like track inside, stem criteria br1_1, br1_2, br1_3); no attachment requirement; `n_20br1 <= n_20mev`; `shw_sp_filled == 1` implies `n_20br1 >= 1`. **After the BDT stages `n_20br1 == 0` cannot occur** (MEASURED: none of the 1,313 events), so the cut `== 1` is the multiplicity veto `n_20br1 < 2`.

## 11. n_20br1: the 160 removed events (`n20br1_failure_taxonomy.csv`, `n20br1_failure_taxonomy.png`)
All 160 have **`n_20br1 >= 2`** (125 with 2, 30 with 3, 5 with 4 at the controlling candidate; every one of the 160 events has exactly one reaching candidate). Exclusive classes: **extra shower rows that are pieces of the signal photon 126 (78.8 %)**, extra shower rows of other signal-interaction particles 17 (10.6 %), extra shower rows of another interaction or cosmic 8 (5.0 %), unattributed 9 (5.6 %); **149 of the 160 have a clean dominant signal-photon object** (the extra object is a second piece). Split: truth 0p 98 / 1p 53 / >= 2p 9; Delta0 97 / Delta+ 63. So `n_20br1 == 1` removes mostly **split single photons that already look clean** (a photon cut into two energetic showers), not hadronic or foreign showers.

## 12. Photon cleanliness through the chain (`chain_cleanliness.csv`, `clean_photon_fraction_through_chain.png`)
Fraction of the surviving events whose signal photon is a clean energetic electron shower: shower stage **67.1 %** (5,423 of 8,080) -> evaluable 67.1 % -> **numu 82.2 %** (4,117 of 5,007) -> **other 91.9 %** (2,556 of 2,781) -> **ncpi0 97.5 %** (1,280 of 1,313) -> nue 97.5 % -> **n_20br1 98.1 %** (1,131 of 1,153). Fragmented photons: 1,622 -> 582 (numu) -> 150 (other) -> 24 (ncpi0) -> 14 (final); typed non-electron 409 -> 89 -> 29 -> 2 -> 2; track 300 -> 84 -> 12 -> 3 -> 2. **The cleaning is done by the three BDTs** (each stage keeps 76 / 62 / 50 % of the clean and 34 / 25 / 15 % of the non-clean events), n_20br1 adds a small share (88.4 % vs 66.7 %). The conditional survival of clean vs non-clean events at each stage (`chain_conditional_survival_by_photon_state.csv`): numu **76.4 vs 33.6 %**, other **62.1 vs 25.3 %**, ncpi0 **50.1 vs 14.7 %**, n_20br1 **88.4 vs 66.7 %** (n = 33 non-clean events entering). The cumulative separation 20.9 % vs 0.8 % of the earlier phases is therefore the product of (0.76 x 0.62 x 0.50 x 0.88 = 21 %) for clean and (0.34 x 0.25 x 0.15 x 0.67 = 0.8 %) for non-clean events.

## 13. Truth-topology dependence of each stage
Stage by stage (section 2): numu has no intrinsic-looking 0p/1p difference in the raw numbers (62.1 vs 63.0 %) but a -10 pp intrinsic disadvantage for truth 1p after adjustment (section 5); `other` has the large raw difference (47.3 vs 63.2 %, Phase 8: carried by vertex anchoring); ncpi0 has a small raw difference (49.6 vs 47.1 %) and truth >= 2p is lower (36.7 %); n_20br1 removes more truth-0p (82.9 %) than truth-1p (91.8 %) events, because the truth-0p photon is more often split (the reco vertex sits on the photon line, Phase 9). **Which topology dependence is inherited from the reconstruction state:** the numu and ncpi0 raw differences are small because two opposite effects cancel; once the reconstruction state (photon cleanliness, anchored vertex, true proton present) is held fixed the BDT-internal topology dependence is **larger** (-10 pp numu, -14 pp ncpi0 for truth 1p vs 0p), **the topology dependence is not a pure reconstruction inheritance** at these two stages; at `other` it is (Phase 8). All differences are observational bookkeeping.

## 15. Natural-CV check (low statistics; `natural_cv_stage_survival.csv`)
117 natural NC-Delta events of the BNB CV: numu: truth 0p 18 / 36 (50 %), truth 1p 23 / 27 (85 %), truth >= 2p 6 / 7; ncpi0: 4 / 9, 8 / 17, 1 / 5; n_20br1: 4 / 4, 8 / 8, ...; the **numu 0p vs 1p difference (Fisher p = 0.007) is nominally discordant with the official sample (62 vs 63 %)**; with 63 events, a different truth-region/selection of the natural sample (117 events inside the rehearsal region) and no adjustment this is **not interpreted**; ncpi0 and n_20br1 are consistent within the statistics.

## 16. What is established
* Exact (3e-9) reproduction of the numu and ncpi0 scores, structure and source of the three BDTs (`FROZEN_*_SOURCE_AUDIT.md`), the dependency graph (numu and other share `numu_score`; ncpi0 consumes 15 nueCC-chain sub-scores), the vacuity of the nue cut, the source semantics of `n_20br1` and its operational meaning (`n_20br1 < 2`).
* The inherited chain turns a 67 %-clean shower-stage sample into a 98 %-clean final sample through the three BDTs; numu rejects compact, few-segment shower objects and events with muon/pion-typed rows, independently of the photon energy; ncpi0 rejects single photons that the reconstruction pairs into a pi0-like candidate (no true pi0 in the sample); `n_20br1` removes split photons.
* The conditional survival of the **clean** signal photon is only 76 % (numu), 62 % (other), 50 % (ncpi0) and 88 % (n_20br1): half of the clean signal events are lost at the ncpi0 stage alone.

## 17. What remains unresolved
Training samples and procedures of the BDTs (not in the XML); whether the compact-shower sensitivity of numu is a training-domain effect (the MicroBooNE muon sample) rather than a property of the physics; which object the `kine_pio_*` pair contains for the 70 % of events with a pair (the pair members were not identified by truth); the intrinsic -10 / -14 pp topology dependences are associations, not causal; the natural-CV numu discrepancy (63 events); 8 inputs per family were not individually source-traced beyond the fill code (the per-input tables are in the source audits); high-energy numu failures were not inspected event by event in the PF tree (the deterministic list of 599 events with dominant attribution is `numu_high_energy_failures.csv`).
