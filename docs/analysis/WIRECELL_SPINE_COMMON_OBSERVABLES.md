# Wire-Cell / SPINE common NC-Delta observables: semantic map (preparation for a same-event comparison)

Date: 2026-11-11. Branch `analysis/ncdelta-common-observables-20261111`. **No SPINE performance number appears in this document.** The Wire-Cell column is source-audited (`NCDELTA_RECO_OBSERVABLE_SEMANTICS.md`, `RECO_PROTON_ROW_SEMANTICS.md`, `DELTA_MASS_DEFINITION_AND_TRUTH_INTERPRETATION.md`). The SPINE column is filled **only from what the earlier SPINE audits established** (`SPINE_SIGNAL_DEFINITION_SOURCE_AUDIT.md`, `SPINE_SIGNAL_DEFINITION_HARMONIZATION.md`, `SPINE_COMPARISON_REQUIREMENTS.md`: the **truth-side** signal and topology definitions, the SPINE primary classification, the proton threshold, the fiducial definitions). The **SPINE reconstruction-side definitions of the observables were not source-audited in this repository**; every such cell is marked **TO CONFIRM** and lists what the SPINE side must supply. Nothing is guessed.

Join key for any same-event comparison: `(run, subrun, evt)` of the official Gen2 sample (Phase 7 harmonization flags).

## What can be compared directly now
* The **truth side**: the signal population, the true proton and photon definitions, the 0/1/2/3+ truth proton multiplicity at 35 and 50 MeV (`proton_tables`, harmonization flags).
* The **structure** of the comparison products: per-proton efficiency states, the multiplicity matrix, the KE-response definition (reco / true of the matched primary), the mass-residual definition and the three truth masses.

## What the SPINE side must supply before any reco-level comparison
The reco photon energy and direction definitions, the interaction vertex, the shower-start dE/dx definition (window, calorimetry), the proton selection (primary flag, PID, reco-KE definition and threshold), the reconstructed neutrino-energy definition and the mass formula, all keyed by `(run, subrun, evt)`; plus the SPINE candidate/interaction policy (no analogue of `any`/`first`).
