# Analysis-readiness blockers: what is still missing for a final signal/background prediction and a data/MC comparison

Date: 2026-11-11. Branch `analysis/ncdelta-analysis-definition-decisions-20261111`. Nothing here is resolved by this phase; this note records the state so that no final number is quoted before the blockers are lifted.

## 1. Final purity, total S/B and significance remain BLOCKED by the cosmic-only (beam-off) normalisation
* The prediction object refuses to produce purity, S/B and significance while a component is unresolved (`prediction.py`, `UnresolvedNormalizationError`; `tables/official_signal/prediction_status.json`); this phase does not change that and **introduces no cosmic normalisation**. The signal rate and the beam-neutrino background rate are quoted independently (per 1e20 POT) under the same reconstructed FV; the ratio of the two is labelled **signal / beam-neutrino-background reference ratio**, never S/B.
* **The blocker is a collaboration decision.** The gate-counter evidence is documented in `DATA_EXPOSURE_NORMALIZATION.md` section 0 (SOURCE: `SBNDBNBEXTRetriever`, `CAFMaker`): open beam-on data (runs 18255, 18259) carry `rec.hdr.nbnbinfo` = 960,047 matched BNB spills and the beam-off sample `rec.hdr.noffbeambnb` = 2,542,847 off-beam BNB gates; the two streams record 9.4 % and 2.0 % of their counted gates, so the gate-counter scale and a recorded-trigger scale differ by a factor ~4.8; the InTime-Run1 beam-off sample has no run-18255 file. Matteo's use of gate counts is referenced there; **neither quotient is formed** until the collaboration confirms the approved scale (question 4 of `QUESTIONS_FOR_HAIWANG_HANYU.md`).
* Depends on D/E/F? Only through the shape of the beam-off component inside the chosen reco FV (4 / 2 / 0 beam-off events pass the frozen selection in the open data); the absolute scale is independent of D, E and F.

## 2. Flash-time offset (~ +0.3 us beam-on versus MC): preserved, not corrected
`FLASH_TIME_CONVENTION_AUDIT.md`: the `wc.T_tagger.flash_time_us` of the in-window candidates peaks later in beam-on data than in MC (median 1.29 us vs 0.97 us); the source shows a per-frame offset in data and none in MC (`ShiftData2MC = 0`); no document states that the offset is expected. **No data shift was applied here.** **No proposed FV, proton or candidate decision depends on the flash time**: the FV and proton definitions use vertices and `T_kine` rows, and the candidate ordering (`nu_index`) uses the activity length, not the flash time; the in-window candidate pool itself is flash-defined, so a timing convention change would change which bundles exist for data and MC alike (question 5).
