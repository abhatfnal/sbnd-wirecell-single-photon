Test the hypothesis that the reco vertex sits at the hadronic vertex rather than at the photon conversion point (Phase 6: reco vertex displaced along the photon direction by about the conversion distance in matched candidates). | the whole 0p/1p `other` gap lives in the detached stratum (`FROZEN_OTHER_BDT_SIGNAL_TOPOLOGY_AUDIT.md` section 7) |

| **O** | **Upstream BDT outputs as inputs**: `numu_score`, `numu_1_score`, `numu_2_score`, `cosmict_10_score` are scores of other BDTs (production-computed). | **P** | **Proton-origin dependence**: confirm whether the decay-proton vs FSI-created difference (67 % vs 54-57 % survival) is a vertex-connectivity effect or independent; consider the ambiguous (35) and decay-proton-vs-knock-out cases with a finer truth chain. | explains the Delta+ vs Delta0 difference inside truth 1p |

| **R** | **Recording-the-tie-convention note**: the frozen scorer sends an input exactly equal to a cut to the left, TMVA/production to the right. No score changed in the studied samples (max 3e-9), but the scorer in `bdt.py` was deliberately left unchanged. A future phase should decide whether to align it (a bookkeeping change, not a physics one). | exactness of the offline score vs production |

## Rules for the next phases
* Characterization before optimization; every result states the denominator, the candidate policy, the proton threshold and the truth region it used.
* A baseline efficiency is never quoted against another analysis's efficiency without the harmonization of `SPINE_SIGNAL_DEFINITION_HARMONIZATION.md`.
* The frozen selection is changed only in item L, and only after the decisions D-H are recorded.
