# Common reconstructed NC-Delta observables in Wire-Cell (official Gen2 signal, BNB CV selected objects)

Date: 2026-11-11. Branch `analysis/ncdelta-common-observables-20261111` (cut from the pushed Phase-10 head `2819310`). **Characterization only**: no mass cut, selection, BDT retraining, threshold or FV optimisation, and **no policy is frozen**. Population: official Gen2 NC-Delta signal, provisional TPC truth box (12,427 events), frozen production fg1 reconstruction and frozen BDT cuts; "final selected" = the 1,153 events of the frozen selection (candidate policy `any`; `first` = 1,101); primary proton convention = type 2212, reco KE > 35 MeV, the existing `included` convention; the two proton **policy sensitivities** are 35 MeV without `included == 3` rows and 50 MeV. Background objects: the 649 passing candidates of the BNB CV (635 background + 14 natural signal-like) with the candidate-associated taxonomy. **Signal uses a truth-region denominator and the BNB objects have no common FV: every signal-vs-background comparison below is a unit-area SHAPE diagnostic of selected objects, never a rate, purity or S/B.** Documents: `NCDELTA_RECO_OBSERVABLE_SEMANTICS.md`, `DELTA_MASS_DEFINITION_AND_TRUTH_INTERPRETATION.md`, `WIRECELL_SPINE_COMMON_OBSERVABLES.md`. Tables `tables/common_observables/`, figures `plots/common_observables/`, objects outside Git `$SP_DATA/common_observables/official_ncdelta/`.

## 1. Motivation
Seokju's SPINE NC-Delta technote, Prabhjot's plotting style and Matteo's truth/reco method point to one set of physically interpretable observables, none of which needs a framework-specific BDT score: photon energy and direction, shower gap, shower-start dE/dx, proton kinematics, the photon-proton opening angle, the vertex, the reconstructed energy and the reconstructed Delta mass. This phase defines them from audited Wire-Cell objects and measures their truth resolution on the official sample.

## 2. Source semantics
(`NCDELTA_RECO_OBSERVABLE_SEMANTICS.md`.) Key facts: `shw_sp_energy` is the charge-based energy of the selected shower object; **no shower direction vector is stored** (only `shw_sp_angle_beam`); the `T_pf` shower node runs from the shower **start point to the farthest trajectory vertex** (the end point is not on the charge), and its start point equals the tagger's start point (MEASURED 3e-7 cm) in the vertex's SCE-corrected frame; `shw_sp_shw_vtx_dis` is **0 whenever the shower's start segment is attached to the main vertex**; the stem dQ/dx vector is MIP-normalised, padded with 3, with pathological first entries (up to 6e9) from `dx ~ 0`; `kine_reco_Enu` is a **visible-energy sum** of every row plus binding/rest terms; there is no per-plane dQ/dx.

## 3. Object definitions
Per final-selected event: the selected candidate (`sel_nu_index`, or candidate 0 for `first`); the selected photon = the electron-typed shower row whose KE equals `shw_sp_energy` (1,151 of 1,153 found; 2 have no matching row), photon truth-correct (top `T_match` level-1 partner is the signal photon) in **1,148 of 1,151** (3 are not); the proton = the unique typed-proton row above the policy threshold (reco 1gamma1p); the leading-proton rule (highest KE, lowest row index) is implemented for >= 2 rows but not used in a headline. Counts (any, baseline policy): **1gamma0p 527, 1gamma1p 556, >= 2p 70**. Row-level truth association for both objects is the Phase-9/10 one. Output: `selected_object_table.parquet` (one row per object, 136 columns incl. every status and truth class).

## 4. Delta-mass definition
`M^2 = m_p^2 + 2 E_gamma (E_p - p_p cos theta)`, `E_p = T_p + m_p`, PDG `m_p = 938.27208816 MeV`, root taken only for `M^2 > 0` (never clipped). Baseline policy: **554 usable** (554 `ok`, 2 `no_photon_row`, 597 not reco 1gamma1p; 0 non-positive); excluding `included == 3`: 503; 50 MeV: 538. (`DELTA_MASS_DEFINITION_AND_TRUTH_INTERPRETATION.md` sections 1, 2.)

## 5. Truth interpretation of the mass
Three masses: **A** generator decay pair (decay photon + decay nucleon before FSI = the Delta record mass), **B** signal photon + post-FSI proton, **C** reco. For Delta0 the decay nucleon is a neutron (6,845 of 6,845), so a reco proton can never be the decay nucleon and `M_reco` is **not** a Delta invariant mass; for Delta+ (5,581 of 5,581 decay nucleons are protons) it equals A only if the final-state proton is the decay proton unchanged by FSI. Generator check (12,426 events): A peaks at **1232 MeV (mode)**, median 1209, 68 % interval 1155-1260, identical for the two charges, equal to the Delta record mass to < 1 keV in 100 %; its shape is asymmetric and **narrower above the peak than a nominal Breit-Wigner** (not fitted). For the 348 selected Delta+ decay pairs, B - A is exactly 0 for most events (validation of the construction) and RMS 28 MeV from the rescattered minority.

## 6. Signal M_Delta (`delta_mass_signal_decomposition.png`)
554 reco 1gamma1p events: **median 1220.8 MeV, 68 % interval 1136-1316, mode ~1225, 87 % between 1100 and 1400**. Pair classes: Delta+ decay pair 348 (62.8 %), FSI-created / other signal primary 143, Geant4 secondary 35, fake / non-proton 28, wrong photon 0. Truth-correct subset (491): median 1220.5, 1138-1310. By proton origin: decay pair 1212 (1137-1285), FSI-created 1240 (1144-1346), secondary 1215 (68.6 % in 1100-1400, 17 % below 1100), fake 1266 (14 % above 1400). By topology: truth 1p 1216 (88.5 % in window), truth 0p 1228 with mode 1125 and 14 % below 1100, truth >= 2p 1262 with 11 % above 1400. By charge: Delta+ 1218, Delta0 1232 (the Delta0 peak is **not** the decay-pair mass). A Delta(1232)-like peak therefore appears for the selected signal as a whole, is cleanest when the proton row is a true signal primary, and is not an exclusive Delta+ signature.

## 7. Mass resolution (`delta_mass_resolution.png`)
Delta+ decay pairs (348): `M_reco - M_true_decay` median +2.8 MeV, 68 % interval [-44, +34] MeV (half-width 39), RMS 65 (7.8 % beyond three half-widths); reconstruction part (C - B) half-width 36.5 MeV. Descriptive substitution study (non-additive): E_gamma true 20.0 MeV; both directions true 26.1; photon direction true 28.5; proton direction true 34.9; **proton KE true 36.1 (no effect)**; E_gamma + opening angle true 1.0 MeV. **The width is set by the photon energy (half-width 10.4 %, bias +3.4 %) and the opening angle (cos half-width 0.14), not by the proton KE (3 %).** A reco-only photon direction (vertex -> shower start, detached showers) lowers the width to 29.1 MeV and shifts the median to +8.1.

## 8. Photon energy and direction (`photon_energy_response.png`)
1,148 truth-correct selected photons (5 excluded as not the signal photon or without a row, not mixed into the resolution). **E_reco/E_true: median 1.034, 68 % half-width 12.7 %**; by true energy 100-200 MeV 1.04 / 13 %, 200-300 1.04 / 12 %, 300-500 1.03 / 13 %, 500-800 1.05 / 11 %; by conversion distance 11-15 % at every distance (0-5 cm 14 %, 40-80 cm 14.5 %); truth 0p 14.1 %, truth 1p 11.8 %; attached 13.7 %, detached 11.2 %; Delta+ 11.5 %, Delta0 14.2 % (`photon_energy_response.csv`). **Direction** (`T_pf` chord): median cos 0.984, median angle 10.2 deg, 68 % within 15.8 deg, 82.9 % with cos > 0.9, **3.0 % negative** (35 events: 89 % attached, median chord 75 cm; 5.3 % for truth 0p versus 0.8 % for truth 1p). The reco-only vertex -> start direction (567 detached showers) has median cos 0.9996 and 97.0 % above 0.9. `shw_sp_angle_beam` minus the true beam angle: median +0.65 deg, half-width 5.6 deg, 10 % beyond three half-widths. No direction is flipped.

## 9. Shower gap and conversion geometry (`shower_gap_vs_conversion_distance.png`)
In-volume conversions with a truth-correct photon (1,140): 50.6 % attached (gap 0). **Anchored vertex (706):** 24 % attached; the detached showers follow the true conversion distance (Spearman **0.975**, median gap minus conversion distance +0.2 cm). **Not anchored (434):** 94 % attached, no correlation (0.007). Truth 0p: 86 % attached (detached rho 0.59, n = 65); truth 1p 24 % attached (rho 0.97, n = 446); true proton row present: 18 % attached (rho 0.97), absent: 80 % attached (rho 0.74). Truth 1p x anchored: 13.6 % attached, detached rho 0.98. **The gap is a conversion-distance estimator only for a detached shower with an anchored vertex; elsewhere it is a vertex-anchoring/topology observable** (Phase 9).

## 20. What is established
* The shower end-point/direction definition (start -> farthest trajectory vertex) and the whole set of observable meanings; no direction vector is stored; directions are not flipped.
* `M_Delta` is computable for 554 of 556 reco 1gamma1p events; the generator Delta+ and Delta0 decay masses peak at 1232 MeV; the truth-level construction reproduces the generator mass exactly for unrescattered protons; the Delta+ decay-pair reconstruction has +2.8 MeV bias and a 39 MeV half-width limited by the photon energy and the opening angle.
* The Delta0 mass peak is not a decay-pair mass; the mass is not an exclusive Delta+ signature.
* Photon energy: +3.4 % median bias, 12.7 % half-width, flat in energy and conversion distance. Photon direction: 10 deg median angle. Proton KE: unbiased above 75 MeV.
* The shower gap tracks the true conversion distance only for anchored, detached showers.
* `kine_reco_Enu` is the visible energy (1.05 of the true visible energy), not the neutrino energy (0.48).

## 21. What remains provisional
Proton threshold (35 vs 50 MeV) and the `included == 3` policy (item E); candidate policy (item F); the photon-direction definition for external use (chord vs vertex -> start); the generator line shape of the Delta mass (narrower than the nominal Breit-Wigner, not understood); the SPINE-side definitions; the BNB-CV statistics (small cells) and absence of data, beam-off and systematics; the 2 selected events without a shower row; the signal denominator (provisional TPC box).
