## 4. V7 membership audit (bookkeeping only; read-only SAM queries used, as authorised)

### 4.1 Method
`scripts/upstream_membership_audit.py` now classifies each official sample against a delivered manifest as **CONFIRMED_IDENTICAL_FILE**, **CONFIRMED_SHARED_GENERATOR_ANCESTRY** (same production job id), **CONFIRMED_GENERATOR_IDENTICAL_EVENTS** (only from delivered evidence), **SHARED_SEED_REQUIRES_VERIFICATION**, **NUMERICAL_COLLISION_NO_ANCESTRY**, **ESTABLISHED_DISJOINT** or **UNDETERMINED_INDEPENDENCE**. Different job ids or different run numbers never establish independence: `ESTABLISHED_DISJOINT` requires data files with no file in common, or a documented distinct generator-seed namespace; `OFFICIAL_SEED_NAMESPACE_DOCUMENTED` is empty because no such documentation exists (tested). Inputs: our manifests, our events tables (run/subrun/evt identifiers only), the saved SAM metadata, and Aurora's `membership_files` / `membership_events` (identifiers and roles; **no event-level record is committed**, only the 4-row aggregate `tables/upstream_release/official_vs_aurora_membership.csv`).

**Why NC-Delta stays UNDETERMINED (highest priority).** The official samples are themselves Aurora productions (SAM `production.type: aurora`, software v10_14_02_05, created 2026-05-25/26), and the Aurora training sample was produced with the same software family. There is no overlap of file names, production-job ids or run numbers, but: (i) the generator seed of the official productions is recorded nowhere we can read: SAM reco1 metadata has no parents, no `fcl` seed, no `NuRandomService` policy (queried: `samweb get-metadata` / `file-lineage parents` on an official reco1 file); (ii) Aurora's NC-Delta samples use `NuRandomService linearMapping`, seed = 10 x (offset + job) + k with offset 75M (generator seeds 750,004,154-780,037,524) and Aurora's νe CC uses offset 0 (seeds 94-34,896,204, instance numbers from 9 up to 3.5 M, overlapping numerically the instance range of the official runs 201-476 x 100 subruns); the offsets of the official productions are unknown, so identical seeds (hence generator-identical events) between official and Aurora samples cannot be excluded or confirmed; (iii) Aurora found 134 groups of generator-identical BNB events from repeated GEN seeds under the `random` policy inside its own BNB sample, with different run/subrun numbers, 60 of which straddle the outer holdout: the same mechanism could in principle link the official BNB to Aurora's BNB. Generator-event identity of official events can only be tested by reading truth content, which is out of bounds.

**Exact manifests needed to close it (bookkeeping, no event arrays):** for each official production (`EX_ncdeltarad`, `EX_nuecc` v10_14_02_05, BNB CV v10_14_02_03): the generation-stage fcl or log extract showing the NuRandomService policy and seed offsets (or per-unit seeds) used; with that, `OFFICIAL_SEED_NAMESPACE_DOCUMENTED` can be filled with a written reference and the verdict becomes ESTABLISHED_DISJOINT or SHARED_SEED_REQUIRES_VERIFICATION per pair. From Snehadri: the DL-vertex training units / per-event fold list.

### 4.3 Upstream and DL-vertex roles and limits
- **Xuyang's roles are reconstructed, not authoritative.** Aurora rebuilt his fit / early-stop pools from his training code applied to Aurora's tables (νμ 209,060 fit + 23,373 early-stop vs his logs 209,294 + 23,395; νe 153,310 + 17,185 vs 153,347 + 17,191). The differences come from PARTIAL files excluded from our tables (256 νμ pool rows in 14 PARTIAL BNB files; 45 νe pool rows), and the small-model pools (`up_numu_l1_pool`, νe level-1 object pools with the rule-A objects) are reconstructed from code, not from his actual fitting populations. They also cannot show which exact files entered each small model. So "official sample is outside upstream training" rests on file/job-id disjointness (no overlap found) and **not** on an authoritative upstream manifest.
- **The Aurora odd-subrun outer holdout is not upstream-independent** (his released models were fitted on most of those rows); only the official samples can be independent, conditional on the seed question above.
- **DL vertex (Snehadri):** trained on production units of aurora-r2 BNB and νe CC (the membership tables flag 301,830 BNB and 265,367 νe CC candidates as sharing units with the DV2-MAX training sample; per-event fold not recoverable), NC-Delta and beam-off not listed. Overlap of these units with the **official** BNB and νe CC samples is UNDETERMINED (no unit list, and the official samples are different job ids from Aurora's).
- Independence statement to carry with any result: upstream variant and cut choices already consulted the Fermilab evaluation productions; the final evaluation is downstream-SP-independent **conditional on the collaboration-selected upstream configuration**, and conditional on closing the seed-namespace question above.

## 5. Beam-off (policy retained)
Ledger `tables/upstream_release/beam_off_membership_ledger.csv` (unchanged): **all 572 odd-run files are excluded** from evaluation (558 staged for SP-other training, confirmed identical to Aurora's `membership_files`, plus the 14 not staged); the **486 even-run files (22,894 events, 76 runs) remain evaluation-eligible**, subject to verification against Xuyang's actual training membership list and eventual authorisation. Normalisation-readiness checklist (all required before any beam-off enters an evaluation; also encoded in the bundle template/validator): (1) the exposure of the **even-run half alone**, recomputed from its own counters (the all-file scale is not reusable); (2) the approved counter convention (gates `noffbeambnb` vs recorded triggers; all-file sums are 2,542,847 gates vs 960,047 beam-on); (3) live-time / dead-time treatment; (4) run coverage compatible with the open data (runs 18255 and 18259; no beam-off file exists in run 18255); (5) approved by a named person; (6) `imp_weight` question resolved for the MC side.
